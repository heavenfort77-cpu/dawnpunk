# Marla's journal (draft lines, for the owner)

Marla's private journal sits on the desk in her house in the base. One new line is unlocked per trip (each completed night run back to base). Line 1 is there from the first base visit. Tone: tsundere: she denies caring, and every line gives her away. T-rated, no romance stated outright. Ashley Potts is "he" and "Potts" (docs/gdd.md).

1. New panel-tender. Potts. Loud boots. Won't last a week.
2. Potts survived. Barely. I only watched because someone has to count losses.
3. He waved from the panels. I didn't wave back. My hand was cold, that's all.
4. Mended my scarf. Not for anyone. It kept getting in my eyes.
5. He came back with scrap and a dent in his helmet. Idiot. I checked the dent twice.
6. Told Oko the panel-tender is replaceable. Oko laughed. Oko is wrong about lots of things.
7. Saved him a pear from the garden. It was going soft anyway. Obviously.
8. His first name is Ashley. Ashley. I say it in my head when he's being stupid. Often.
9. Stood on the wall till dawn. For the view. The view is very good from there.
10. Potts took a hit meant for the turbine. I yelled. Loudly. For the turbine.
11. Stitched his glove while he slept by the stove. Bad stitching costs us power. Strategy.
12. Bram asked if I like Potts. I threw a bean at him. At Bram. Not Potts.
13. Stopped writing about Potts in the scouting log. Started this journal instead. Unrelated.
14. He laughed at something I said. It wasn't a joke. Still counts.
15. Long night. He came back. That's the entry.
16. Pip drew Potts and me holding hands. Confiscated it. Evidence. It's in my drawer.
17. He fell asleep on the bench. Put a blanket on him so he'd stop shivering. The noise, you know.
18. If he gets hurt out there, who do I complain to? Exactly. Keep him alive, Marla.
19. He calls me "Scout" like it's a compliment. It isn't. I'm not smiling. Shut up, journal.
20. Made two cups of tea by mistake. Left the second by his door. Also a mistake.
21. Counted the Gloom tonight. Counted Potts too. Both came back. I'm glad about one of them.
22. I'm not waiting at the gate. I'm guarding it. Very closely. Every dawn.
23. He left a note: "thanks, M." Keeping it. Recycling matters.
24. Potts asked what I'm writing. "Gloom counts." He believed me. Good. Idiot.
25. The turbine hummed all night because he fixed it. Good job. I said it to the turbine.
26. If anyone reads this: none of it means anything. Especially the Potts pages.
27. Dreamt the dawn didn't come. He was there anyway, fixing panels. Woke up annoyed.
28. Fine. He's brave. Stupidly brave. I can admit that here, where nobody looks.
29. I'd follow that idiot into the dark. I mean lead. Obviously I'd lead.
30. Dawn again. He's alive. I'm... fine with that. Very fine. Shut up.

After line 30, every further trip repeats a short closing line: "Same as always. He came back. Good."
