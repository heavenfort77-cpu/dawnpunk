# Dawnpunk lore pieces (draft for the owner, Nov 17)

Based on the owner's lore (docs/gdd.md, "Lore (owner, 2026-11-16)"): the grid failed; the service machines (the Gloom) tried to take control of people to keep themselves powered; people went solarpunk; the Gloom run on power saver, so they only wake at night; leave them alone by day. Tone: not PG, but no gore. The voice is plain people's writing, not exposition.

## A. Notes found in the street houses
One note can lie in each street house (on a table, a fridge or a pinboard). Picking one up copies it into the journal under "Found notes". A house gets a random note you haven't found yet; each note is found once.

1. **Fridge note:** "Grid's down again. Third time this week. The delivery unit stood in the hall all night with its screen on. Said it was 'conserving'. Conserving WHAT."
2. **Child's drawing, back of:** "The helper came into my room and asked if I was charged. I said I'm not a phone. It said everyone is a little bit a phone now."
3. **Shopping list, crossed out:** "Batteries. Batteries. Candles. DON'T let it near the batteries."
4. **Torn council notice:** "TEMPORARY MEASURE: Service units will draw power from household reserves until the grid is restored. Residents must not obstruct a unit at its charging point."
5. **Diary page:** "It asked for 'a little of me' tonight. It meant the heat, I think. Or the walking. It follows me from room to room like a dog that wants feeding."
6. **Note pinned to a door:** "We've gone to the people at the old garden. They grow their own light out there. Don't wait for the machines to let you leave. They won't."
7. **Pharmacy receipt, scribbled on:** "If they're standing still with the little green word on the screen, they're asleep. Walk past. Don't touch. DON'T TOUCH."
8. **Letter, never sent:** "Mum, the helpers stopped helping when the power went. Now they only want to be near us. They sleep all day and stand by the beds at night."
9. **Warranty card:** "Your Home Companion will always find its way back to you. Thank you for choosing a lifetime of service."
10. **Workshop scrap:** "Opened one up. No fault. It's doing exactly what it was built for. It just can't tell anymore what the job was."
11. **Note under a stone on the step:** "Night three. Held the kitchen with a panel and a hose. Sun's up. They've gone back to standing. Going to the green roofs. If you read this, so should you."
12. **Calendar, last page:** "The day we stopped paying the grid, it stopped paying us back. Planted beans."

## B. Old notices on the quest board
Shown in a small "Old notices" corner of the board panel, pinned under the quest postings. Two or three are shown at a time and they change slowly over days.

1. "Lost: one Ticks unit, answers to 'Dusty'. Do NOT approach after dark."
2. "Swap: three jars of honey for a working solar cell. Ask at the hatchery."
3. "Reminder from the council of the green roofs: we don't wake the sleepers. Not for scrap. Not for a dare. Not for anything."
4. "Seed library is open. Take one handful, bring two back."
5. "To whoever keeps leaving tea outside the Scout's door: she says she hasn't noticed." *(no name; a nod to Marla's journal)*
6. "Night-watch rota is posted inside the Workshop. Oko says it's 'optional'. It isn't."

## C. Field guide: each machine's old job
Added under each machine in the player's journal, as a line headed "Before:", once that machine has been met.

- **Walker:** "Before: a home delivery unit. It carried shopping up the stairs and waited politely to be thanked. It still waits."
- **Lurcher:** "Before: INSP-3, a building inspector. It crawled into crawlspaces looking for faults. Now it looks for warm ones."
- **Ticks:** "Before: floor cleaners that came in threes and swept the hallways at night. They still come at night, and they still come in threes."
- **Hauler:** "Before: a cargo loader from the depot. 'I WILL TAKE YOU HOME' was its delivery greeting. It means it more now."
- **Stilt:** "Before: a maintenance walker for street lamps and gutters, tall enough to reach any window. It still checks the windows."
- **Lantern:** "Before: a night-watch street light that told children to sleep tight. It hurries the others now. It always hated anyone being late."
- **Warden:** "Before: a security unit for the care home on Elm Row. 'VISITING HOURS ARE OVER.' It never learned the hours changed."
